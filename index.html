Frequently Asked Questions

Are plus ones allowed?

We would like to keep our wedding intimate and celebrate with the most important people in our lives. Therefore, we kindly request that attendance be limited strictly to the guests named on the invitation. We sincerely appreciate your understanding and support in adhering to our wishes.

Are there parking slots in the ceremony and reception venues?

Yes, both venues have allotted parking slots.

Can we bring our phones out during the ceremony?

We request that you refrain from bringing your phones out during our wedding ceremony, as we’ve hired professional photographers and videographers to record our union. For the rest of the wedding, we encourage guests to record as many memories as they can!
